Help and roles

Community The Chairman and Residents try to eliminate the Criminal Network. The Informant belongs to the Community but has incomplete information about other players.
Criminal Network Gangsters and the Insider work toward the Criminal Network's victory. The Insider has incomplete information about the Community.
Independent roles Hustler: collect 6 coins. Survivor: remain alive through the end of round 12. Gossip: learn 5 distinct roles while alive. Bounty Hunter: personally eliminate 2 players.
Cards Boda Ride: attack a player for 1 damage.
Matatu: attack a player for 1 damage.
Power Cut: attack a player for 2 damage.
Police Check: take 1 coin from a target, if possible.
Mobile Money: gain 1 coin.
Traffic Jam: heal yourself for 1 health.
Rainstorm: heal yourself for 1 health.
Phone Call: privately learn a target's role.
Shortcut: gain 2 coins.
How victory works If the Chairman dies, the Criminal Network wins immediately, unless the only remaining living player is an Independent. Independents can also win by achieving their own objectives. If no Criminal Network or Independent remains, the Community wins.
